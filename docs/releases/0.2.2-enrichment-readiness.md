## Educational and content limits

Existing authored historical text, canonical answers and history/tradition distinctions are reused. No new historical facts, dialogue or custom TV distractors were generated. Shivneri's birthplace context and Rajgad's capital context were spot-checked against [Maharashtra Tourism's Shivneri page](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/fort/shivneri/) and [Rajgad page](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/fort/rajgad/); the Pratapgad date was spot-checked against [its official tourism page](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/fort/pratapgad/). This is not a comprehensive historical audit.

Selection alone, exploration, self-report, helped practice and a fresh check do not establish later recall. Individual later card recall requires the same card's prior independent witness, a different session and authored prompt, and at least 24 hours. TV defaults to shared family recognition and never creates that individual witness. Optional discussion is not recorded or assessed. No child study, recording collection or personal-data export is part of this work.
