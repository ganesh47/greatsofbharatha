Directional learning sentences derive from the authored coordinates; distances are straight-line approximations.

The local equirectangular projection corrects longitude at each board's central latitude and uses one uniform points-per-kilometre scale. Pairwise projection distances are checked against spherical great-circle distances with a 1% tolerance. Regional boards and close-up views have independent kilometre scales; decorative terrain does not define place positions.
