## Held or excluded material

- **birth-year-and-age** — State schoolbooks and tourism use 1630; the ICOMOS evaluation uses 1627. Keep existing commemorative wording; do not derive an exact age.
- **torna-exact-date** — Existing curation records 1646/1647 uncertainty. Do not quiz the exact capture year or age sixteen.
- **rajgad-duration-and-diameter** — Tourism page’s capital duration, capital move date, and base diameter are not sufficiently reliable for this pass.
- **treasure-pots-and-divine-causation** — School retelling combines a treasure story with devotional attribution; do not present divine causation or a pot count as established history.
- **agra-basket-method** — Teach custody, safe return, and resilience. An exact escape method is not needed and should not become an unquestioned fact.
- **twenty-seven-in-four-months** — The Standard Seven text explicitly attributes this count to chronicler Sabhasad. Do not turn a reported figure into an unqualified quiz total.
- **fort-and-fleet-totals** — Approximate or reported totals are not suitable fixed-answer counts. Count only clearly bounded named features or offices.
- **throne-mass-and-workshop-count** — No need for uncertain measurements or a single-source workshop total; they add memorisation without useful understanding.
- **invented-dialogue** — Dramatized speeches and the fictional guide are not eyewitness historical sources.
- **unesco-twelve-forts** — Twelve is a modern World Heritage serial-property count, not the total number of forts held in Shivaji’s reign.
